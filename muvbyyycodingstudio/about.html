About the studio

We started Muvbyyy because handoffs were the problem.

Too many software projects lose momentum at the seams — between the agency that designed it, the contractor who built it, and whoever's left holding it once it's live. We built a studio that stays in one piece from the first sketch to the five-hundredth deploy.

What we believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

01

One team, start to finish

The engineers who design your system are the ones who operate it after launch.

02

Small, senior teams

We'd rather staff a project with three strong engineers than eight junior ones.

03

Written down, not tribal knowledge

Specs, runbooks, and decisions live in documents your team can read without us in the room.

04

Boring technology, on purpose

We reach for proven tools by default, and only use something new when it earns its place.
